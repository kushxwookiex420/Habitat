# DropPilot — HOTO AutoCare Footage Permission Request

## Goal
Obtain written permission to use real HOTO AutoCare Air Duster & Vacuum demonstration footage in DropPilot AI's commercial TikTok/social-commerce product video.

## Why this gate exists
A publicly viewable product video is not automatically licensed for reuse in an advertisement. Do not download, trim, upload, or publish brand or creator footage until the rights holder confirms permission for the intended use. Search results and an action label are not proof of rights.

## Official product reference
- Product: HOTO AutoCare Air Duster & Vacuum
- Official product page: https://hototools.com/products/hoto-autocare-air-duster-vacuum
- Official support/contact page: https://hototools.com/pages/contact-us
- Published support email: service@hototools.com
- Official IP page (for IP concerns, not general permission requests): https://hototools.com/pages/intellectual-property-rights

Ask the brand to route the request to its marketing/content/licensing team. Do not assume customer support is the rights holder.

## Message to send

Subject: Permission request — HOTO AutoCare product demo footage for DropPilot AI

Hello HOTO team,

I'm preparing a short product-demonstration video for DropPilot AI's TikTok Shop and social channels featuring the HOTO AutoCare Air Duster & Vacuum.

Could you please confirm whether HOTO can provide approved product-in-use video clips or grant written permission to edit and use specific HOTO-owned demonstration footage in a commercial social-commerce video?

The planned edit would show the actual product vacuuming debris, blowing dust, and using its attachments. It may include a short product introduction, factual captions, narration, and a closing call to action. The video would be posted to TikTok and potentially other DropPilot-owned social channels to promote the product.

Before using any footage, please confirm in writing:
1. Which exact video files or URLs are approved.
2. Whether editing, trimming, vertical cropping, captions, voiceover, and promotional calls to action are allowed.
3. Which platforms and territories are covered.
4. Whether attribution, logo use, or any required disclaimer is needed.
5. Whether the permission has an expiration date or other restrictions.

If HOTO has an official media kit or affiliate/creator asset library with commercial-use terms, please point me to it. I will not reuse public footage until the permission and applicable terms are clear.

Thank you,
DropPilot AI

## Approval evidence to retain
Do not mark footage approved until the project records:
- The exact source URL/file and rights holder.
- A copy or durable reference to the written permission/license.
- Approved platform(s), commercial purpose, edit rights, territory, term, and attribution requirements.
- Human reviewer confirmation that the clip actually shows the labeled action and the actual product.

## Fallback if permission is not granted
Use original footage recorded by an authorized product owner, supplier-provided assets with explicit commercial terms, or another source with a clear license covering commercial edits. Do not substitute unrelated generic vacuum footage and present it as the HOTO product.
