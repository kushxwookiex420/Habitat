# DropPilot product-demo video standard

A product demo is not approved just because an MP4 was rendered.

## Required source footage
- Use real footage of the actual product in operation, not a slideshow or generic b-roll.
- Record the direct media source, title, selected timestamps, action category, and documented commercial reuse permission.
- Include at least two distinct action categories. The caller-supplied action label is metadata, not proof; a reviewer must inspect the clip and final render.
- Never treat an official brand video as automatically reusable for a commercial advertisement. Confirm the specific license or written permission first.

## Vertical edit presentation
- Deliver 1080×1920 vertical video with the full frame intentionally composed.
- Show the product name briefly at the beginning rather than keeping a large headline over the whole demonstration.
- Show a compact action badge on each clip: vacuuming, blowing, or attachment in use. Keep it in a consistent safe region and ensure it does not cover the product's working area.
- Keep the closing call to action visible only during the final 3.5 seconds. The CTA must not obscure the demonstration for the entire video.
- Narration must fit within the selected footage duration; do not silently speed up or cut off the final sentence.
- Render completion is not publish approval. Inspect playback, audio, action accuracy, caption readability, rights, and CTA before a person approves posting.

## Current implementation limits
The renderer now times the product title to the opening 3 seconds, action badges to their individual clips, and the CTA to the final 3.5 seconds. It does not yet perform computer-vision proof of product action, guarantee permanent artifact storage, or establish commercial rights. Do not claim those capabilities until separately implemented and tested.
