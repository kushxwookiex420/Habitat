Habitat Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

Habitat is an AI-assisted productivity and content orchestration application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Habitat and connect third-party services.

2. Information handled

Depending on the features you use, Habitat may handle account identifiers, content you provide, task and workflow information, technical information needed to operate the service, and authorization information for connected services.

3. Connected services

When you connect a third-party service, Habitat uses the permissions you authorize to provide the requested integration. Habitat does not claim ownership of your third-party content. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms.

4. AI processing

Information submitted to AI-assisted features may be processed by the AI providers configured for the Habitat service in order to generate, summarize, transform, research, or organize content.

5. Security and retention

Habitat takes reasonable measures intended to protect information used by the service. Retention depends on the feature and operational requirements. Users should not submit information they do not have permission to process.

6. User choices

You can stop using connected integrations and revoke authorization through the relevant third-party service. You may also contact the project owner through the Habitat project repository with privacy questions.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as Habitat evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed through the Habitat project repository.
