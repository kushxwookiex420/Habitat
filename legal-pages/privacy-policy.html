Habitat Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how Habitat may handle information when you use its AI-assisted productivity and content-management features. The actual information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account and contact details: information you provide, such as an email address.
  • Tasks and content: prompts, task details, drafts, and media or other content you choose to submit.
  • Connected-platform data: information made available by a third-party platform after you authorize a connection.
  • Technical information: service logs, error details, and basic device or request information used to operate and secure the service.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide requested features, coordinate AI-assisted tasks, connect authorized services, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and improve reliability. Information is not used to publish content to a connected account unless you invoke an available feature and the required authorization is in place.

3. AI and service providers

Some features may send the information needed to fulfill your request to AI providers or hosting and infrastructure providers. Their processing may be governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit sensitive information unless it is necessary and you understand the applicable provider’s practices.

4. Connected platforms

If you connect TikTok or another platform, Habitat processes only the data made available through the permissions and APIs involved. The platform independently handles data under its own policies. You can revoke access through the platform’s account settings where supported.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers supporting Habitat, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect rights, safety, and service integrity. Habitat does not sell personal information for money. This policy does not promise that every provider stores or processes data in the same way.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described above, operational requirements, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Some temporary connection data may remain in service memory only for the duration supported by the current implementation.

7. Your choices

You can stop using Habitat, avoid submitting optional information, and revoke connected-platform permissions where available. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use, contact us below. We may need to verify your request and retain certain records where legally required.

8. Children

Habitat is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or the rules of connected platforms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when revisions are posted.

10. Contact
