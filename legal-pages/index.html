AI-assisted creator operations

Make content work easier to manage.

Habitat Ax is a workflow workspace for creators and small teams. It helps organize research, plan scripts and media, track production tasks, review generated artifacts, and understand the status of connected services—all while keeping external publishing under user control.

Open Habitat workspaceHow the workflow works

Built around the full content lifecycle

Habitat separates drafts, review artifacts, approvals, and confirmed external actions so teams can tell what has actually happened.

Research and planning

Organize a topic or product brief into a research checklist, hook, script outline, captions, and a scene plan. Unsupported claims should be flagged rather than presented as facts.

Production and quality checks

Track generated media, narration, branding, task status, and automated audio/video checks. Automated checks support review; they do not replace human judgment.

Connected-service diagnostics

Review connection and permission status for supported services. Access depends on the account owner’s authorization, provider API access, platform eligibility, and service configuration.

A transparent workflow

1

Plan

Define the goal and collect relevant source material.

2

Create

Prepare scripts, captions, and a media plan; generate a review artifact where the tools and source rights permit it.

3

Review

Check factual claims, source rights, product accuracy, sound, visuals, and platform requirements before use.

4

Authorize and approve

Grant only the required account permissions and explicitly approve the specific external action. A saved token or successful render alone does not mean a post is authorized or published.

5

Verify

Use the provider’s response to distinguish a successful action from a queued, failed, or unconfirmed attempt.

Privacy and account access
