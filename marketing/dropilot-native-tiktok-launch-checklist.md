## Automated release gates
The render workflow must fail unless:
- MP4 exists and is non-empty.
- Video is exactly 1080x1920 (vertical).
- Audio and video tracks both exist.
- The rendered duration is long enough to contain the complete audio track (no fixed 30-second cutoff).
- The video is at least 20 seconds and larger than 100 KB.

A passing technical gate is not the same as human visual approval. Watch the full MP4 before posting and confirm: narration finishes naturally, product is visible and not obscured, all text fits inside safe areas, captions match the narration, and the ending has a clean final beat.

## Native TikTok posting handoff
Until TikTok authorization and required API audit are approved, do not claim Habitat has posted publicly or try to bypass the authorization/audit. Use the finished MP4 through TikTok's native create/upload flow, review the preview, set the desired audience, add accurate product link/product tag where available, disclose commercial content as required, and tap Post yourself.

Before posting, check current TikTok requirements and the account's available product-linking features. Do not burn a logo, watermark, URL, or promotional overlay into a video intended for TikTok API Direct Post unless the applicable TikTok policy explicitly permits it.

## Final checks before publishing
1. Re-check supplier stock, shipping destination, delivery estimate, return terms and current landed cost.
2. Calculate contribution margin using actual supplier cost + shipping + platform/payment fees + expected refunds/returns; don't rely on Shopify inventory alone.
3. Confirm the product page is active and the product can actually be purchased.
4. Watch the exported video from beginning to end with sound on.
5. Review caption, product tag/link, commercial disclosure and audience settings in TikTok.
6. Publish only after the human review; then record the post URL and early performance metrics (views, average watch time, clicks, add-to-carts, orders).
