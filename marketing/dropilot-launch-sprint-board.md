## Decision rule
- Do not label a product a winner based on views alone.
- Re-check supplier landed cost, inventory source, shipping, return policy, marketplace fees, payment fees and expected returns.
- Compare click-through and add-to-cart rates as well as purchases.
- Increase production effort only after there is evidence of purchase intent or profitable orders.

## Publishing integration constraint
TikTok's current Content Posting API rules require approved authorization for relevant scopes, user consent and a compliant posting interface. Unaudited Direct Post content is restricted to private viewing. Until that status is confirmed, use the native TikTok posting flow and do not claim Habitat posted publicly.
