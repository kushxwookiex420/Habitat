## First-week test plan

1. Publish only after product-page and margin checks pass.
2. Create two hooks from the same verified footage: (A) “Crumbs in every crevice?” and (B) “A closer look at a compact car-cleaning tool.”
3. Use the same product link and comparable posting windows; don't change several variables at once.
4. Record baseline views, 3-second hold/retention if available, profile visits, product-link clicks, add-to-carts, and orders after 24 hours and 72 hours.
5. If views but no clicks: improve the offer/product page and CTA. If few views: test a stronger first-second visual/hook. If clicks but no carts: investigate price, shipping, trust, and product-page clarity. Don't infer success from views alone.
6. Don't buy fake engagement or claim testimonials, test results, discounts, or scarcity that aren't real.

## Storefront checklist before traffic

- Confirm the public storefront is not password-protected / “opening soon.”
- Verify this product is active and its product URL opens without logging in.
- Verify supplier stock, fulfillment cost, shipping times, return terms, and variant availability.
- Calculate contribution margin after product cost, shipping, platform/payment fees, discounts, and expected refunds.
- Make sure the listing has clear original/authorized media, accurate specs, and a visible purchase button on mobile.
- Add a working contact/returns page and consistent DropPilot branding.
- Add campaign UTM parameters to the final product URL where supported, then test it on a phone.

## Approval gate

No automatic public posting. A human must approve the final rendered MP4, its captions, the destination URL, and the current product economics.
