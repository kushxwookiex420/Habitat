AI-powered content orchestration.

Habitat helps users research, create, organize, review, and publish digital content through a single AI-assisted workspace and connected services.

What Habitat does

  • Researches and organizes content ideas and source material.
  • Uses AI-assisted workflows to draft, transform, and review content.
  • Coordinates tasks and worker services through the Habitat workspace.
  • Connects to supported third-party services when the user authorizes those integrations.
  • Supports content publishing workflows, including TikTok integration where approved and authorized.
